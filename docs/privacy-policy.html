Privacy Policy for Breathe

Last updated: August 04, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Breathe mobile application ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it.

Summary: Breathe does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and the App does not transmit to us or anyone else:

  • Your name, email address, or any other contact information
  • Your location
  • Photos, contacts, or files on your device
  • Analytics or usage tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any data at all, over the network

The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server. It functions entirely offline.

Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the App stores the following data locally on your device, using Hive, an on-device local storage library:

  • Session history — the date, time, duration, and completed cycles of your breathing sessions
  • Mood check-ins — how you felt before and after a session, if you choose to record it
  • Preferences — your selected breathing technique, ambience sound, and app settings (sound, haptics, theme)

This data is stored in app-private storage on your device. It is never uploaded, synced, or made accessible to us or any third party. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage/data from your device's system settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Permissions

The App may request the following device permission:

  • Vibration — used to provide haptic feedback during breathing phase transitions (e.g. a gentle pulse when you should inhale or exhale). This permission does not involve access to, or collection of, any personal data.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage beyond its own private app data.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. It does not make network requests as part of normal operation.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Health Disclaimer

Breathe is intended for general relaxation and wellness purposes only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Please consult a healthcare provider for medical advice.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the App adds an optional feature that involves data leaving your device — we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
